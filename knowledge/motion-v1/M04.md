# GSAP animation architecture and builder license boundary
Original DevKiller research note, 2026-09-20. Research-only; GSAP is NOT installed or enabled for generated applications.
GSAP offers timelines and scroll-driven choreography. Review its official docs for sequencing concepts, scoped cleanup and responsive animation. However the current Webflow product terms include restrictions on competing visual animation builders. DevKiller includes visual editing, so this integration needs an explicit licensing determination before production enablement. A general claim that GSAP is free for commercial use is not enough to establish builder permission.
The generator must not import gsap, fetch a GSAP CDN, or claim GSAP capability while this gate remains closed. Use installed Motion for state/layout or scroll progress and Anime.js for multi-step DOM/SVG timelines. Preserve natural scrolling, content visibility, reduced motion, cleanup and keyboard access. Do not mix animation owners on the same element/property. This research entry is useful to choose an allowed alternative, not to authorize a new dependency. Do not turn lack of GSAP into failure to create the application.
Sources: https://gsap.com/docs/v3/ ; https://webflow.com/legal/product-terms (section 9, GSAP)
